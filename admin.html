🚀 Switch to Real M-Pesa Payments

You are currently on Sandbox (test mode). Follow these steps to go live and collect real Ksh 250 payments from students.

Step 1 — Apply for Go Live on Daraja
  1. Go to developer.safaricom.co.ke → click Go Live in left sidebar
  2. Fill in your business details — you need a registered business or M-Pesa Paybill/Till number
  3. For a personal Till: go to any Safaricom shop with your ID and register a Lipa Na M-Pesa Till (free)
  4. Submit the Go Live form — Safaricom reviews in 2–5 business days
  5. You will receive your live Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, Shortcode and Passkey via email
Step 2 — Update Vercel Environment Variables

Once you have your live credentials, go to vercel.com → nest-finder-cuk → Settings → Environment Variables and update:

MPESA_ENV = live
MPESA_CONSUMER_KEY = your live key
MPESA_CONSUMER_SECRET = your live secret
MPESA_SHORTCODE = your Till/Paybill number
MPESA_PASSKEY = your live passkey
MPESA_CALLBACK_URL = https://nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app/api/mpesa-callback
Step 3 — Get Supabase Service Key

Go to supabase.com → nestfinder-cuk → Settings → API. Copy the service_role key (the secret one). Add it to Vercel as SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY. This lets the callback function confirm payments automatically.

Step 4 — Redeploy on Vercel

After updating all variables, go to Vercel → Deployments → Redeploy. Students will now pay real money and contacts unlock automatically.

💡 Until then: Students pay in sandbox (test mode). You manually confirm payments from the Payments tab above. Contacts still unlock correctly.
📧 Email Notifications — When Students Pay

To get email alerts when a student pays, add this to your mpesa-callback.js Vercel function after the payment is confirmed:

// Send email via Resend (free tier — 100 emails/day)
// 1. Sign up free at resend.com, get API key
// 2. Add RESEND_API_KEY to Vercel env vars
// 3. Add this after payment is confirmed:

await fetch('https://api.resend.com/emails', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    'Authorization': 'Bearer ' + process.env.RESEND_API_KEY,
    'Content-Type': 'application/json'
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    from: 'NestFinder CUK <noreply@nestfinder.co.ke>',
    to: ['waren23greg@student.cuk.ac.ke'],
    subject: 'New Payment — Ksh 250 received',
    html: `<p>A student just paid Ksh 250.<br>Ref: ${mpesaRef}<br>Phone: ${phone}</p>`
  })
});
🌐 Custom Domain — nestfinder.co.ke
  1. Buy nestfinder.co.ke at KENICregistry.or.ke or truehost.co.ke (~Ksh 1,500/yr)
  2. In Vercel → Settings → Domains → Add nestfinder.co.ke
  3. Vercel shows you DNS records to add — go to your domain registrar and add them
  4. SSL certificate is automatic — site goes live on your domain in ~10 minutes
  5. Update MPESA_CALLBACK_URL in Vercel to https://nestfinder.co.ke/api/mpesa-callback
🔍 SEO & Google Discovery

Help students find NestFinder CUK on Google. These steps are free and take under 30 minutes.

Step 1 — Submit to Google Search Console
  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console → Add property
  2. Enter nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app (or your custom domain)
  3. Verify ownership via HTML tag — add the tag to your index.html <head>
  4. Submit sitemap: nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app/sitemap.xml
Step 2 — Add these meta tags to index.html <head>
<meta name="description" content="Find affordable student housing near The Cooperative University of Kenya. Single rooms Ksh 3,500–5,500, Bedsitters Ksh 6,000–10,000, One bedrooms Ksh 10,000–13,000. Verified listings, no scammers.">
<meta name="keywords" content="CUK student housing, Cooperative University Kenya rentals, student bedsitter Karen, single room Rongai, student accommodation Langata">
<meta property="og:title" content="NestFinder CUK — Student Housing">
<meta property="og:description" content="Safe verified student housing near CUK. Browse free, pay Ksh 250 to unlock caretaker contact.">
<meta property="og:url" content="https://nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app">
<meta property="og:type" content="website">
<meta name="robots" content="index, follow">
Step 3 — Create sitemap.xml

Create a file called sitemap.xml in your project root:

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<urlset xmlns="http://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9">
  <url><loc>https://nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app/</loc><priority>1.0</priority></url>
  <url><loc>https://nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app/login.html</loc><priority>0.8</priority></url>
  <url><loc>https://nest-finder-cuk.vercel.app/payments.html</loc><priority>0.6</priority></url>
</urlset>
