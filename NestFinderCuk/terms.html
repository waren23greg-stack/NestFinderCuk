01

About NestFinder CUK

NestFinder CUK is a student-run housing directory platform that helps students of The Cooperative University of Kenya (CUK) find safe, verified, and affordable rental housing near campus.

We are not a real estate agent, landlord, or property manager. We are a listing platform that connects students with caretakers of verified properties. We do not own any of the listed properties.

NestFinder CUK is not affiliated with The Cooperative University of Kenya, Safaricom, M-Pesa, or any government institution.

02

Eligibility

NestFinder CUK is intended for students currently enrolled at or applying to The Cooperative University of Kenya. By creating an account you confirm that:

  • You are a student, prospective student, or guardian of a CUK student
  • You are at least 18 years of age
  • The information you provide during registration is accurate and truthful
  • You will use the platform only for finding legitimate student housing
03

Our service

NestFinder CUK provides:

  • Free browsing — all listings, photos, prices, and locations are visible to everyone at no cost
  • Caretaker contact unlock — for a one-time fee of Ksh 250 per listing, we reveal the caretaker's phone number so you can arrange a viewing directly
  • Verified listings — all listings are personally verified by the NestFinder CUK admin before being published
We are a directory, not a rental agency. Once you have the caretaker's contact, all rental agreements, deposits, and tenancy arrangements are directly between you and the caretaker or landlord. NestFinder CUK is not a party to any rental agreement.
04

Payments — Ksh 250 fee

The Ksh 250 contact fee is a one-time payment per listing that gives you permanent access to the caretaker's phone number for that specific listing.

  • Payment is made via M-Pesa to NestFinder CUK admin number 0704 285 315
  • You submit your M-Pesa confirmation code on the platform
  • Admin verifies the payment and your contact is unlocked — usually within a few minutes
  • The Ksh 250 fee covers the cost of listing verification, platform maintenance, and admin support
  • The fee does not include any rent, deposit, or caretaker fee
The Ksh 250 is not a house deposit. It is purely a platform fee for accessing contact information. Any deposit or rent is paid separately and directly to the caretaker or landlord.
05

Refund policy

The Ksh 250 contact fee is non-refundable once the caretaker's contact has been revealed to you. This is because the service — providing contact information — has been delivered in full.

Refunds may be considered in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Payment was made but the contact was never unlocked due to a platform error
  • The caretaker's contact information is incorrect or the number does not exist
  • The listing was removed or marked as taken before your contact was unlocked

To request a refund, contact admin on WhatsApp within 24 hours of payment with your M-Pesa confirmation code. Approved refunds are processed within 48 hours.

06

Listings & accuracy

All listings on NestFinder CUK are personally verified by the admin before publication. We make reasonable efforts to ensure that listing information — including prices, photos, and locations — is accurate at the time of publication.

However, rental prices, availability, and property conditions can change. NestFinder CUK does not guarantee that:

  • A listed property will be available when you contact the caretaker
  • The price shown is the final price offered by the landlord
  • The property condition matches the photos at the time of viewing

We encourage you to always view a property in person before making any rental payments or signing any agreements.

  • Any disputes between tenants and landlords or caretakers

Safety reminder: Always bring a trusted friend when viewing a property. Never pay a deposit or rent before signing a formal rental agreement. If anything feels unsafe or suspicious, contact us immediately on WhatsApp.
